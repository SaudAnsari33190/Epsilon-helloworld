01 // Initialization

Not just building robots.
Building the future.

As a core part of the Science & Technology Council, Epsilon is where theoretical engineering meets raw hardware. You do not need to be an expert to join us, but you do need the zeal to unlock the potential of technology.

From drafting 2D CAD models for laser cutting to programming ESP32 microcontrollers, we provide the platform, the funding, and the tools to bring your ideas into reality.

02 // Active Modules

What we actually do.

Embedded Systems

Writing C, C++, and MicroPython for microcontrollers. We bridge the gap between software logic and physical electrical components.

Hardware Fabrication

Designing mechanical specifications for robots, generating DXF files, and wiring relay-based H-bridge motor drivers.

Competitive Robotics

Building specialized machines for flagship events like Robo-Carnival. From autonomous maze-solving to heavy-duty Robo Soccer.
