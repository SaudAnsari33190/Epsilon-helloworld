01 // Initialization

From Blueprint to Reality.

Epsilon operates at the intersection of mechanical fabrication and embedded systems. As the flagship robotics division of the Science & Technology Council, we provide the workspace, machinery, and funding to transform CAD assemblies into functional machines.

Whether it is prototyping custom chassis, tuning differential steering mechanisms, or integrating high-torque actuators, this is where theoretical engineering meets raw hardware. Experience is not mandatory; a drive to build physical systems is.

02 // Active Modules

What we build.

Mechanical Design & Fabrication

Drafting complex kinematic models using Fusion 360 and other softwares. We take parts from screen to workshop—leveraging 3D printing for rapid iteration and utilizing hands-on manual prototyping to shape and assemble custom structural components.

Control Systems & Power

Designing the nervous system of our machines. This covers wiring high-current relay-based H-bridge motor drivers, integrating ESP32 microcontrollers, and tuning closed-loop feedback to achieve precise physical control over mechanical joints.

Competitive Robotics

Engineering durable, high-performance machines for flagship arenas like Robo-Carnival. From autonomous terrain rovers to heavy-duty Robo Soccer platforms, we build systems designed to outmaneuver and outlast the competition.

03 // Connect

Open to All. Built for the Best.

Epsilon is an open community. We conduct regular, hands-on workshops throughout the year for anyone interested in robotics—no official membership required.

Core Member Benefits

Official members gain exclusive access to club hardware, the right to represent IIT Bhilai in external competitions, and a dedicated senior mentor to accelerate their technical development.
